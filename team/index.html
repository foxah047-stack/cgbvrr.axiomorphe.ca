The CGBVRR brings together coordination, project management, field monitoring and technical support roles. This structure presents the team according to real responsibilities without imposing an overly rigid organizational chart.Le CGBVRR réunit des rôles de coordination, de gestion de projets, de surveillance terrain et de soutien technique.
