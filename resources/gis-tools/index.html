Outils SIG

GIS Tools

Online GIS and mapping resources that support watershed information, environmental data review, and regional planning around the Restigouche watershed.

These external tools are provided as public reference resources. Data availability, map layers, and access conditions may change on the source websites.

Mapping Resources

Public tools for exploring watershed and geographic information

The CGBVRR shares links to online GIS and mapping resources that can help users explore watershed information, environmental context, aquatic data, and regional planning information.

These tools can support a clearer understanding of rivers, streams, catchment areas, land context, and other geographic information relevant to the Restigouche watershed and surrounding region.

What These Tools Support

Watersheds
Viewing rivers, streams, catchments, and related aquatic information.
Planning
Finding public geographic data that can inform local and regional decisions.
Research
Locating maps and datasets that support education, analysis, and environmental understanding.

NB Aquatic Data Warehouse

A mapping and data resource focused on aquatic and water-related information in New Brunswick. It helps users view maps, explore watershed information, and access data related to rivers, streams, and catchment areas.

Open Tool

GeoNB

A provincial geographic data portal from the Government of New Brunswick. It provides access to maps, datasets, and location-based information that can support planning, research, education, and environmental understanding.

Open Tool

Using These Tools

Residents, partners, researchers, and organizations can use these resources to better understand the Restigouche watershed, nearby aquatic systems, and the broader geographic context of the region. They are intended as reference tools and should be interpreted alongside current field knowledge, official guidance, and project-specific information.
