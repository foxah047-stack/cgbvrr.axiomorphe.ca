extra_body_en: |
  New surveillance equipment has been installed at several priority salmon pools across the watershed as part of our resource protection program. Remote-trigger cameras document illegal fishing activity without requiring permanent staff presence on site.

  ## Why now

  With Restigouche salmon populations at their lowest levels in two decades, every fish matters. Poaching pressure — even at low intensity — can have a disproportionate impact on spawning stock when populations are this low.

  Monitoring data from our field teams and partner First Nations communities suggests some pools see illegal activity during the run.

  ## Our approach

  Cameras are installed at access points identified as high-risk, in coordination with:

  - The Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP)
  - The Sûreté du Québec
  - NB and Quebec wildlife protection officers
  - The Listuguj Mi'gmaq Government

  Footage is reviewed regularly and passed on to the relevant authorities when an infraction is detected. Exact equipment locations are not made public, for operational reasons.

  ## Report an observation

  If you observe suspicious activity on the Restigouche River or its tributaries, you can contact:

  - **RCMP Campbellton**: 506-789-7000
  - **Sûreté du Québec — Matapédia**: 1-888-310-1122
  - **NB Poaching line (toll-free)**: 1-800-565-1227
