summary_title: A long-term strategy for cold-water refuge protection
summary: >-
  WaterShade is a long-term, watershed-scale approach for identifying, protecting, enhancing, and monitoring cold-water refuges in the New Brunswick portion of the Restigouche River watershed.
intro: >-
  Cold-water refuges are cooler areas of a river system that can help Atlantic salmon during warm-water periods. WaterShade connects science, field validation, restoration planning, land-use discussions, and public communication into one coordinated strategy.
context_show: true
context_title: Why this work matters
context_body: >-
  Warmer summers and changing river conditions can increase thermal stress for cold-water fish. In those conditions, cooler pockets of water, shaded tributaries, groundwater inputs, and protected riparian areas become important parts of salmon habitat resilience. WaterShade helps organize that work at the scale where river systems actually function: the watershed.

key_concept_show: true
key_concept_title: Cold-water refuges are small places with watershed-scale importance
key_concept_body: >-
  A cold-water refuge may look modest from the riverbank, but its protection can depend on a much larger drainage area, including forests, headwater streams, riparian vegetation, groundwater movement, land ownership, and management practices. WaterShade treats these refuges as connected features, not isolated points on a map.
process_show: true
process_title: How the strategy works
process_intro: >-
  The public page should explain the process without publishing sensitive technical details. The full work involves inventory, prioritization, field validation, protection planning, restoration, monitoring, and communication with partners.
steps:
  - title: Identify and organize data
    text: Compile existing information on thermal anomalies, cold-water inputs, stream conditions, and previous fieldwork.
  - title: Prioritize areas
    text: Use a consistent framework to select cold-water refuges and drainage basins where protection or restoration can have the strongest value.
  - title: Validate in the field
    text: Confirm site conditions through field visits, temperature monitoring, drone imagery where appropriate, and technical review.
  - title: Protect, restore, and monitor
    text: Prepare site-specific actions, support riparian restoration, coordinate with land managers, and follow results over time.
